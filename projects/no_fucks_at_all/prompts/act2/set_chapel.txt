A wide shot of a tiny low-budget television studio dressed as a crude little wedding chapel. Two wobbly white pillars made of painted cardboard tubes with lopsided paper capitals stand either side of a small plywood altar draped with a lace tablecloth and a vase of plastic flowers. Behind them a plywood flat painted as a stained-glass window in hot pink, turquoise and lemon yellow, a white paper runner carpet on the floor, a single row of folding chairs with white bows, paper wedding bells and streamers taped overhead. At the edges of the frame the real studio shows: the unpainted plywood backs of the flats, black drape curtains, a lighting grid of fresnel lamps overhead, and cables across the scuffed grey concrete floor.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, flat bright studio lighting from overhead fresnels, hugely saturated candy colours.
