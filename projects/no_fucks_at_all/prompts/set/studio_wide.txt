A high wide establishing shot of an entire small 1980s public-access television studio, seen from a raised corner behind the audience area. In the left foreground, four rows of simple wooden bleachers for a studio audience run diagonally away from camera, empty. Across the studio floor beyond them, three separate low-budget sets stand side by side in a row, each built from painted plywood flats with the unpainted backs of the flats showing at their edges: on the left a cartoon bedroom set with a big hot pink cloud-shaped headboard, a polka-dot bed and purple and yellow zigzag walls; in the centre a band stage with a black-and-white checkerboard riser, a red drum kit and amplifiers in front of a hand-painted cartoon city skyline in turquoise, hot pink and lemon yellow with a jagged lightning bolt; on the right a cartoon office set with canary yellow walls, painted-on windows and a fire-engine red desk. Behind the sets, the back wall of the studio is hung with huge swathes of brightly coloured stage curtains in hot pink, tangerine orange and turquoise. A lighting grid packed with fresnel lamps hangs overhead. Three boxy vintage television cameras on wheeled pedestals stand on the scuffed grey concrete floor aimed at the sets, with thick cables snaking between them.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, bright studio lighting, hugely saturated candy colours.
