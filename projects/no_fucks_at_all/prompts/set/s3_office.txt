A wide shot of a tiny low-budget television studio dressed as a cartoonishly cheap office. Plywood flats painted canary yellow with flat painted-on windows showing a turquoise sky and a cartoon skyline, a lopsided desk painted fire-engine red with an oversized chunky beige desk telephone, a stack of paper taller than the desk, a bright blue filing cabinet with one drawer hanging open, a giant painted wall calendar and a potted plastic palm. The floor is scuffed grey studio concrete with gaffer-tape marks. At the edges of the frame the real studio shows: black drape curtains, a lighting grid of fresnel lamps overhead, and a boxy studio camera on a wheeled pedestal at the left edge with cables across the floor.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, flat bright studio lighting from overhead fresnels, hugely saturated primary and candy colours.
