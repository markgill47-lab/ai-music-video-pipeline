A wide shot of a tiny low-budget television studio with a band stage built in it. A short plywood riser painted with a black-and-white checkerboard carries a red drum kit, two small guitar amplifiers and a chrome microphone on a stand. Behind it, a hand-painted plywood backdrop of a cartoon city skyline in turquoise, hot pink and lemon yellow, with a giant jagged lightning bolt and wobbly painted stars, the brushstrokes and plywood seams plainly visible. At the edges of the frame the real studio shows: black drape curtains, a lighting grid packed with fresnel lamps overhead, a boxy studio camera on a wheeled pedestal at the left edge, thick cables snaking across a scuffed grey concrete floor, a strip of gaffer tape marking the floor.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, flat bright studio lighting from overhead fresnels, hugely saturated primary and candy colours.
