A wide shot of a tiny low-budget television studio dressed as a cartoonishly cheap bedroom. A double bed with a big hot pink headboard cut from plywood in a wobbly cloud shape and a turquoise-and-yellow polka-dot bedspread, two lumpy pillows, a lime green bedside table on each side with a chunky orange lamp, plywood walls painted with purple and yellow zigzag wallpaper, a cardboard window showing a painted navy night sky with a big smiling crescent moon and stars, a shaggy magenta rug and a plastic alarm clock. The floor is scuffed grey studio concrete with gaffer-tape marks. At the edges of the frame the real studio shows: the unpainted plywood backs of the side flats, black drape curtains, a lighting grid of fresnel lamps overhead, and a boxy studio camera on a wheeled pedestal at the right edge with cables across the floor.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, flat bright studio lighting from overhead fresnels, hugely saturated primary and candy colours.
