A wide shot of a tiny low-budget television studio dressed as a surreal cartoon living room. Wobbly hand-built furniture in clashing patterns: a lumpy lime-green armchair with purple polka dots, a zigzag-striped coffee table, a giant papier-mache lamp shaped like a question mark, a wall clock with crooked oversized numbers, and walls painted in tilted stripes of orange, turquoise and magenta with cut-out cardboard windows showing a painted blue sky. The set sits on a checkerboard linoleum floor. At the edges of the frame the real studio shows: the unpainted plywood backs of the side flats, black drape curtains, a lighting grid of fresnel lamps overhead, and a boxy studio camera on a wheeled pedestal at the right edge with cables across the floor.
Photorealistic, 1980s public-access television, shot on 35mm film, fine grain, flat bright studio lighting from overhead fresnels, hugely saturated primary and candy colours.
